## Privacy Policy Last updated: 2026-08-25 This application is a personal tool developed and used only by its developer. It is not distributed to, or used by, the general public. **What data this app handles** - TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens, obtained via TikTok's official Login Kit, used solely to authorize video uploads to the developer's own TikTok account. - Video files and associated metadata (title, description, tags) that the developer selects locally on their own computer for upload. **How this data is used** - Solely to upload the developer's own videos to the developer's own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API. **Data sharing** - No data collected or processed by this app is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Data is sent only to TikTok's official API endpoints as required to perform the upload. **Data storage** - OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's own computer and are not transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's official API. **Contact** - yousuke37@gmail.com
